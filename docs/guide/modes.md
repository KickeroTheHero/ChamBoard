# The four modes

Tap the mode name at the top of the keyboard to switch. Some text boxes limit which modes are allowed; for example, a password box never lets its text be rewritten. The keyboard shows when that has happened.

| Mode | Your draft is hidden | Your style is tidied | Your rhythm is disguised | Good for |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Full Cloak** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Anonymous posts, forums, anything you don't want traced back to you |
| **Biometric Shield** | ✅ | — | ✅ | Messages where your wording must stay exactly as you wrote it |
| **Draft Guard** | ✅ | — | sent all at once | Search boxes, web addresses, passwords, short forms |
| **Passthrough** | — | — | — | Games, number pads, anything that needs every key instantly |

## What the website sees in each mode

- **Full Cloak:** nothing while you write. Then your tidied text, typed in at a random human pace.
- **Biometric Shield:** nothing while you write. Then your exact text, typed in at a random human pace.
- **Draft Guard:** nothing while you write. Then your exact text, all at once, like a paste. Search boxes get an **Emit & Go** button that sends and searches in one tap.
- **Passthrough:** every key as you press it, like a normal keyboard. There's no protection in this mode.

## Pace options (Full Cloak and Biometric Shield)

- **Typed:** letter by letter, like someone typing normally.
- **Glide:** word by word, like someone using swipe typing. It's quicker, and gives away even less.
- If a long message would take too long letter by letter, the keyboard switches to Glide and tells you.

## Emergency bypass

Press and hold the mode name to drop straight to Passthrough for the current text box. It switches back on its own when you move to a different box.
