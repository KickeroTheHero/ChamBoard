# What ChamBoard can't protect you from

No tool gives complete anonymity. Here is what's outside this keyboard's reach.

- **Motion sensors.** Websites can sometimes read your phone's motion sensors, which pick up tiny movements from each tap, even while you type into the private notepad. **What to do:** in your browser's site settings, block "Motion sensors".
- **What you actually send.** Once you press Emit, the app has your text. The keyboard hides *how* you wrote it, not *what* you wrote.
- **What you write about.** Tidying style doesn't hide your opinions, your topics, or facts only you would know. Those can still identify you.
- **Memory copies.** The keyboard erases its drafts, but a phone's memory can briefly keep copies that aren't under the keyboard's control. Treat erasing as "very likely gone", not "guaranteed gone".
- **Masks are educated guesses.** The habits a mask picks from (how many people write "lol", use British spelling, and so on) are currently estimates, not measured from real writing samples.
- **Leaving mid-typo.** If you switch to a different text box while the keyboard is replaying a deliberate typo, that one stray letter can stay behind in the box you left.
- **Noticing the tool.** A careful website might notice that text arrives in whole words or all at once. Glide pace is designed to look like ordinary swipe typing, which millions of people use. The project's own test page can already tell ChamBoard apart, because most phone keyboards show each word being built on screen before it's committed, and ChamBoard doesn't.
- **Your phone itself.** If your phone or your browser is compromised, or someone can see your screen, a keyboard can't help.
- **Typos that are real words.** If you type "its" for "it's" or "cant" for "can't", the keyboard can't tell it's a mistake, so it leaves it. Those slips can still be recognisable.
- **Languages.** Style tidying currently works for English only. Other languages are sent unchanged, but still get draft hiding and rhythm disguise.
